How feedback reshapes the recommendation

Every comparison a person makes is folded into the reward model, in order, starting from the weights fitted offline on simulated preferences. Nothing here is stored state — the model is replayed from the event log each time this page loads, so this is how it actually got here.

The step size applied per comparison, and how hard the model is pulled back toward its offline prior. Larger steps make a handful of comparisons visible in the ranking; they do not make the result more reliable.
